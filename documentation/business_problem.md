# Business Context & Problem Statement - ProcureFlow

## 1. Enterprise Profile
**GlobalProcure Corporation** is a fictional multi-national enterprise operating across 6 geographic regions:
- **North America**
- **Europe**
- **Asia-Pacific**
- **Latin America**
- **Middle East**
- **Africa**

The company manages a global supply chain network of **150 active vendors** providing over **600 unique products** across 8 core product categories:
1. Raw Materials
2. Electronics
3. Heavy Machinery
4. Office Supplies
5. Logistics Services
6. Packaging & Storage
7. IT Hardware
8. Chemicals

Procurement operations are decentralized across 8 specialized functional departments (Operations, Supply Chain, IT Operations, Manufacturing, Facilities, R&D, Logistics, Corporate Admin). Annual procurement spend exceeds **$345M ($698.7M total over the 2-year 2024–2025 timeline)** across more than **37,800 purchase orders**.

---

## 2. Executive Operational Challenges
Senior Leadership at GlobalProcure Corp has identified severe operational friction and margin erosion across its purchasing lifecycle. Key management complaints include:

1. **Escalating Procurement Spend**: Total purchasing expenditures have grown rapidly without proportional gains in output quality or delivery speed.
2. **Chronic Delivery Delays**: Operational teams report frequent stockouts and project delays caused by late vendor shipments.
3. **Supplier Performance Disparities**: Lack of standardized vendor rating scorecards has allowed underperforming suppliers to retain lucrative contracts.
4. **Severe Vendor Concentration Risk**: Informal buyer preferences have concentrated an overwhelming share of order volume among a small subset of dominant vendors.
5. **High-Value Order Bottlenecks**: Critical and high-priority orders regularly experience lead time spikes during peak seasonal periods (Q3/Q4).
6. **Regional Operational Inconsistencies**: Significant variance in fulfillment performance and lead times across global operating regions.
7. **Pricing Inefficiencies**: Repeated purchases from expensive spot-market vendors despite active primary contracts.

---

## 3. Core Business Questions
Executive Leadership has engaged the Business Analytics / Consulting team to perform an empirical SQL and data-driven analysis to answer **12 vital business questions**:

1. **Vendor Scorecard**: Which vendors are performing exceptionally well, and which are underperforming across delivery and quality metrics?
2. **Delivery Bottlenecks**: Which specific vendors exhibit the highest delivery delay frequencies and average lead times?
3. **Regional Cost Distribution**: Which geographic regions and business units consume the highest proportion of total procurement spend?
4. **Operational Bottlenecks**: Where in the procurement lifecycle (Purchase Order Creation $\rightarrow$ Delivery $\rightarrow$ Invoice Payment) are delays concentrated?
5. **Vendor Spend Dominance**: Which vendors account for the largest proportion of total procurement spend?
6. **Price vs. Reliability Trade-Off**: Do premium-priced vendors provide superior on-time delivery performance compared to budget suppliers?
7. **Category Cost Drivers**: Which product categories and subcategories contribute most significantly to annual procurement costs?
8. **Delay Rate Benchmark**: What exact percentage of overall purchase orders are delivered past contractually expected dates?
9. **Lead Time Baseline**: What is the company-wide average door-to-door procurement lead time?
10. **Targeted Vendor Audit**: Which specific vendors or product categories should management target first for contract renegotiation or supplier rationalization?
11. **Concentration Exposure**: Are there signs of extreme vendor concentration risk that threaten supply chain resilience?
12. **Actionable Recommendations**: What concrete operational improvements can reduce delivery lead times and lower procurement spend?
